What PU polish is

PU polish is a transparent polyurethane coating for wood and veneer. It protects the surface from scratches, stains and moisture while letting the natural grain show. A natural polish keeps the wood's own tone. A coloured polish adds a tint, such as walnut or wenge.

How PU polish is applied

  1. Sanding: the grain is opened and smoothed.
  2. Filling: pores, holes and cracks are made good.
  3. PU sealer: two coats, sanded between.
  4. Clear topcoat: matte, glossy or a natural "invisible" look.

PU polish vs melamine polish

PU is more durable than melamine, holds its sheen longer and resists yellowing and cracking better. Melamine is cheaper and gives a basic level of protection.

Can old teak doors be re-polished with PU?

Yes. The old polish is sanded back, holes and cracks are filled, two coats of PU sealer go on, and the door is finished with a clear matte or glossy topcoat.

What is the difference between PU polish and PU paint?

PU polish is clear or lightly tinted and keeps the wood grain visible. PU paint is pigmented and covers the surface in a solid colour.

How do I care for a PU polished surface?

Wipe with a soft microfibre cloth and mild soap. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive pads and standing water, and let the finish cure fully before heavy use.
