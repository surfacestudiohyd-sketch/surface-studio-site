Prices are per finished face, before 18% GST. The per running ft rate applies to panel edges.

How we measure

  • Per sq ft: length × width of each panel, for every face that gets PU.
  • One side or both: factory lists mark panels BSL (both sides lacquered) or OSL (one side, laminate behind). Shutters are usually BSL and carcass sides OSL.
  • Design work: panels with grooves, profiles, fluting or other design work are charged at 1.5× the plain rate.
  • Per running foot: the edges, measured around the full perimeter of each panel, charged in addition to the area.

Worked example

A wardrobe shutter of 2100 × 450 mm is 10.17 sq ft per face and 16.73 running ft around its edges. Painted on both sides in Sirca matte PU, that is 20.34 sq ft × ₹320 + 16.73 rft × ₹100 = ₹8,183 before GST.

Questions

What is the PU paint rate per sq ft in Hyderabad?

Surface Studio charges ₹300 to ₹340 per sq ft for solid-colour PU paint, depending on brand and finish, per finished face and before 18% GST. Axalta metallic is ₹400 and pearl is ₹500.

What is the PU polish rate per sq ft in Hyderabad?

Surface Studio charges ₹210 to ₹245 per sq ft for PU polish, depending on brand, sheen and whether it is natural or coloured, before 18% GST.

Is GST included in these prices?

No. All rates are before 18% GST.

Do designed panels cost more?

Yes. Panels with grooves, profiles, fluting or other design work are charged at 1.5 times the plain-panel rate, because they take more sanding and spraying.

What is the per running foot charge?

It covers the edges. Each panel edge is sanded, sealed and sprayed, so we measure the distance around every panel and charge it per running foot, in addition to the area.
