PU finishing for modular factories and interior firms

How it works

  1. Share the cutlist. Factory lists with part name, material, length, width, quantity and barcode work as they are.
  2. Get an estimate. Upload the same file in the instant quote for a price and quote code.
  3. Deliver the panels to our studio in Bollaram Industrial Area (IDA Bollaram).
  4. We finish and track. Each panel is checked against the list, finished on one or both sides and inspected.
  5. Batch dispatch. Panels go back grouped by unit, such as wardrobe, TV unit or loft.

BSL and OSL

We read the material code on your list. BSL means both sides lacquered, so both faces are finished and charged. OSL means one side lacquered, with laminate on the back, so one face is charged.

What we check on arrival

  • Panel count and sizes against the list
  • Thickness in the material name against the listed thickness
  • Edges and board condition before coating

Anything that does not match is flagged to you before work starts.

Rates

PU paint from ₹300 and PU polish from ₹210 per sq ft, per finished face, before 18% GST. Edges are charged per running foot, measured around each panel. See the full rate card.

Questions

Which file formats can I upload for a quote?

Excel (.xlsx) or CSV with length, width and quantity columns. Factory lists with CLENG, CWIDTH and CNT headings work as they are. For a PDF list, send it to us on WhatsApp.

How do you price panels finished on one side?

Panels marked OSL are charged for one face. Panels marked BSL are charged for both faces.
