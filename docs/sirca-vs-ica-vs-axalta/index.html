Our usual choice for hard-wearing kitchen and wardrobe shutters. Sirca 2K PU lays down a dense, even film in matte or glossy paint and natural or tinted polish.

ICA

Premium Italian PU for interiors where the finish is the feature: smooth matte paint, deep gloss, and veneer polishes that keep the grain crisp.

Axalta

The range we offer in metallic and pearl, and our value pick for solid matte and glossy shades.

How to decide

  • Kitchen or wardrobe in a solid colour: Sirca, or Axalta to save a little per sq ft.
  • Veneer or a statement piece: ICA.
  • Metallic or pearl fronts: Axalta.

Questions

Sirca, ICA or Axalta: which is best for kitchen shutters?

All three suit kitchens. Sirca is our usual choice for hard-wearing kitchen shutters, and Axalta is a good-value option for solid colours.

Which brand is cheapest?

At our rates, Axalta is the lowest for solid colours: ₹300 per sq ft for matte PU paint, against ₹320 for Sirca and ICA.

Which brand do you use for metallic or pearl finishes?

Axalta. Metallic and pearl are not on our Sirca or ICA rate card.
