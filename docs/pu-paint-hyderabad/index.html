What PU paint is

PU paint is a two-component polyurethane coating. It is mixed with a hardener, sprayed in thin layers and cured into a hard, smooth film. Pigmented PU covers the board in a solid colour, in a matte or glossy finish.

How a PU painted shutter is built up

  1. Board: smooth, stable MDF or HDHMR.
  2. Sanding and edge sealing: faces and edges closed off.
  3. PU sealer and primer: built up and sanded level.
  4. Colour coats: shade-matched PU.
  5. Finish coat: matte or glossy.

For kitchens and humid areas we recommend HDHMR over standard MDF, with every face and edge sealed.

Is PU paint better than Duco?

For most interiors, yes. PU is more durable, keeps its gloss longer and is more flexible, so it is less likely to yellow, fade or crack than Duco. It costs more and needs skilled spray application.

Can PU paint be applied over laminate?

Yes, once the laminate is properly prepared with a specialised adhesion primer. Any peeling or swollen laminate has to be repaired first.

Will white PU paint turn yellow?

Standard PU can yellow slightly over time. For whites and pale shades, ask us about the most colour-stable option when you request your quote.
